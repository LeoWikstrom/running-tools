RunTour

Long-press the map (or Set start) to place a start, then Generate. Drag the markers to move them; without a finish the course is a loop.

Pan the map to where you ride and list the bus and tram lines there. Picking one previews it on the map; choose the stops to ride between and create the tour — the places along the way and their texts follow by themselves.

    The browser preview needs the screen on; the Android app runs with it off.
