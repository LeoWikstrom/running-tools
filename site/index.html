running-tools

Small web tools for runners. Free, client-only, no accounts.

  • RunTour

    An audio guide for your run: generate a route, pick up places along the way with texts from Wikipedia, and let GPS trigger the narration as you pass them.

    Open the app Download Android APK

  • Profile Course

    Training for a hilly race? Upload the race GPX, pick a start point near home, and get a runnable course whose elevation profile matches the race.
